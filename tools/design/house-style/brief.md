# House style brief

The style a design is critiqued against. Its tokens: [tokens.css](tokens.css).

## Principles

1. **Calm.** Nothing moves, flashes or shouts unless the person caused it or must act on it now.
   Motion is short (120–220 ms) and explains where something came from; it is gone under reduced
   motion.
2. **Compact, not cramped.** Dense rows and 32-px controls for people who use the tool every day,
   with spacing on the 4-px grid so the density stays even.
3. **Content first.** The person's work fills the sheet; navigation, toolbars and status sit at the
   edges and get out of the way.
4. **Quiet chrome.** Neutral grounds and sheets, thin dividers, few borders, soft shadows only for
   what floats. Hierarchy comes from type weight, size and space, not from boxes and colour.
5. **One accent.** Indigo marks the one primary action and the current selection, nothing else.
   Status colours appear only where a status is shown, always with a word or an icon beside them.
6. **Accessible by default.** Text meets 4.5:1 and controls 3:1 against what they sit on, focus is
   always visible, every target is at least 24 px, layouts survive text at 200 %, and every
   control has a label.

## References

- **Linear**, an issue tracker: dense lists, keyboard first, one accent on neutral greys.
- **Things**, a task manager: calm, generous space around the content, motion that explains.
- **Raycast**, a command launcher: compact rows, quiet chrome, everything reachable by keyboard.
- **iA Writer**, a writing app: the text is the interface, chrome all but gone.
